About

I am passionate about taking on engineering problems and developing solutions to solve them: schematic and layout to board, complex scoring routines on a competition robot, and experimenting with new methods.

I build electrical systems with Cornell Electric Vehicles to engineer hyper-efficient autonomous vehicles for the Shell Eco-marathon Americas, interned on robotics hardware at a Y Combinator (S25) and NVIDIA Inception backed robotics startup in SF, and spent three years developing competitive robotics software at Space Cookies (team partnered with NASA + Girl Scouts). I study Electrical & Computer Engineering at Cornell.

Projects

Hardware & PCB

Front Auxiliary I/O Board, Cornell Electric Vehicles 2026

An I/O board for the front of the competition vehicle. It has a 48 to 12 V buck, controls the lighting systems and horn, and powers sensors and reads their data.

Learn more →
FOC Current Sense Board, Cornell Electric Vehicles 2026

A current-sense board with isolated closed-loop transducers instead of shunt resistors, so there are no shunt losses on the 48 V line, and it lets the team move from trapezoidal control to field-oriented control.

Learn more →
48 to 12 V Buck Converter, Cornell Electric Vehicles 2025

A standalone 48 to 12 V buck converter built around a TI LM5013, used for the horn on the competition vehicle. I did the schematic, layout, soldering, and bring-up in Altium.

Learn more →
Analog Design & Characterization, Cornell ECE Labs 2026

A sixth-order op-amp bandpass filter that pulled a hidden word out of a modulated audio signal. I also reverse-engineered unknown RLC boxes and characterized R-2R and binary-weighted DACs.

Robotics & Control

Robot Bring-Up & Teleoperation, Robotics Center of Silicon Valley 2026

I found and fixed motor, circuit, and wiring faults on the custom M1 Mobile Manipulator robot, then teleoperated more than six robot platforms, including arms, hands, and gloves, for live demos.

OpenArm Electrician & Data Collection, Robotics Center of Silicon Valley 2026

I assembled, wired, and tested OpenArm robots over CAN, and built a data-collection pipeline. I checked each unit before demos and shipments.

Bimanual Inverse Kinematics, Robotics Center of Silicon Valley 2026

An inverse-kinematics module that coordinates a two-arm system, used for teleoperation and data collection.

FRC Robot & Autonomy, Space Cookies · FRC 1868 2022–25

As a programming director of a team of more than 50, I wrote command-based control software: PID and feedforward tuning, autonomous scoring from odometry, and anti-scrub drivetrain control. We qualified for Worlds twice.

Digital & Software

Two Function Calculator + 16-Bit Adder Datapath on FPGA, Cornell ECE 2300 2026

Verilog adders (gate-level, carry-select, and RTL) synthesized in Quartus and compared on area and timing. I verified them with directed, randomized, and X-propagation testbenches.

Tagalong Robot Builder, Space Cookies Robotics 2024–25

An open-source CLI that generates robot control code from mechanism specs, which saved weeks of setup during build season.

Front Auxiliary I/O Board

Cornell Electric Vehicles · 2026

  • Altium
  • CAN bus
  • Synchronous buck
  • Board bringup

Designed and brought up a distributed front-end I/O hub, replacing the centralized auxiliary board architecture and reducing cross-vehicle wiring to power and CAN.

  • Integrated CAN-based lighting/horn control and sensor feedback for steering angle and wheel RPM.
  • Designed a high-current 48 V to 12 V synchronous buck converter to power the horn, using external MOSFETs to handle current demands beyond what integrated regulators could support.
  • Derived switching frequency, inductor, and capacitor values from controller design equations; implemented downstream 5 V and 3.3 V LDO rails.
  • Selected robust sensors, including a Hall-effect absolute steering encoder that retains angle without homing after power loss and inductive wheel-speed sensors.

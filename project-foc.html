FOC Current Sense Board

Cornell Electric Vehicles · 2026

  • Altium
  • FOC
  • Current sensing
  • Power electronics

Designed a three-phase current-sensing board to support the drivetrain's transition from trapezoidal control to field-oriented control (FOC).

  • Selected isolated, closed-loop current transducers that measure phase currents in place of shunt resistors, preventing shunt losses to the 48 V power path.
  • Prioritized high bandwidth and low latency to support accurate, cycle-by-cycle current feedback for FOC.
  • Integrated a shared precision voltage reference across all three channels to maintain consistent offsets and improve measurement alignment for Clarke/Park transforms.

FOC Current Sense Board

Cornell Electric Vehicles · 2026

  • Altium
  • FOC
  • Current sensing
  • Power electronics

A three-phase current-sensing board that moves the drivetrain to isolated, low-latency phase-current sensing for the transition to field-oriented control.

Motivation

I designed the current-sensing board that enables our team's transition from trapezoidal commutation to field-oriented control (FOC), which reduces torque ripple and improves motor efficiency. FOC runs Clarke and Park transforms on all three phase currents every PWM cycle to regulate torque and flux independently, so its performance depends on how cleanly those currents are measured.

Sensing approach

I evaluated and rejected sensorless back-EMF estimation, which fails at standstill, and shunt resistors, which add power loss and pick up switching noise. Instead, I chose closed-loop current transducers that the phase wires pass straight through. This isolates the measurement from the 48 V power stage, adds no loss to the drive path, and has enough bandwidth that sensing adds negligible lag to the current loop.

Shared reference

All three channels share a precision voltage reference, so their offsets track together and don't skew the transform.

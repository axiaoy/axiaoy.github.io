About

I design circuit boards, bring up robots, and write the software that runs them.

I like taking an engineering problem from schematic and layout all the way to a working board — and the control software that runs on top of it. I build electrical systems with Cornell Electric Vehicles for the Shell Eco-marathon, interned on robotics hardware at a YC- and NVIDIA-Inception-backed startup in SF, and spent three years writing competitive robotics software at Space Cookies.

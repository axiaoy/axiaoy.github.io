48 to 12 V Buck Converter

Cornell Electric Vehicles · 2025

  • Altium
  • Buck converter
  • Power electronics
  • LTspice

Designed a standalone synchronous buck converter to power the competition vehicle's horn.

  • Created the full schematic and PCB layout in Altium.
  • Soldered and reworked the board; validated power delivery, ground planes, signal behavior, and loaded operation.
  • Used thermal imaging to monitor component temperatures and verify thermal performance under load.
  • Designed flexible power-rail connections to support clip-on connections and direct soldering.

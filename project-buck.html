48 to 12 V Buck Converter

Cornell Electric Vehicles · 2025

  • Altium
  • Buck converter
  • Power electronics
  • LTspice

A standalone 48 V to 12 V buck converter, taken from schematic through layout, build, and bring-up.

Design

The converter is built around a TI LM5013 (LM5013DDAR) with a Würth 7447706330 power inductor, an MBRS3100T3G Schottky, and ceramic plus polymer output filtering. A 100 kΩ / 11 kΩ feedback divider sets the 12 V output, and the on-time and enable resistors configure the regulator from the 48 V input.

Build and validation

I created the full schematic and PCB layout in Altium, then soldered and reworked the board and validated power delivery, ground planes, signal behavior, and loaded operation. I used thermal imaging to monitor component temperatures and confirm thermal performance under load, and designed flexible power-rail connections that support both clip-on and direct-solder hookups.

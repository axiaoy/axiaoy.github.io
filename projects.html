FOC Current Sense Board

2026

Cornell Electric Vehicles

A current sense board for field-oriented control. It uses isolated closed loop transducers instead of phase shunts, which keeps shunt losses out of the 48 V power path, and shares one precision reference across channels. Isolated, low-latency phase-current sensing is often used in industrial motor drives, and this board facilitates transitioning from trapezoidal control to FOC.

  • Altium
  • FOC
  • Current sensing
  • Power electronics
